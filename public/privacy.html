Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective for all global users

1. Privacy Principles

At City Life, we believe that urban safety and navigation should never come at the expense of your personal privacy. Our architecture is built on a local-first model: your GPS positions, search queries, and route preferences are processed locally within your browser or device wherever possible.

2. Data We Collect & How It Is Used

  • Geolocation Data: When you tap "Auto-Detect GPS" or enable SafeRoute navigation, your device coordinates (latitude and longitude) are sent to Open-Meteo for live weather and OSRM for walking directions. We do not store or track your continuous movement history on our servers.
  • User Accounts: Account profiles, saved spots, and scout rank are stored in your device's persistent local storage. When you sign up, your email and pseudonym are used exclusively to identify your session.
  • Crowdsourced Citizen Reports: Reports you submit (such as hazard alerts or hidden gems) contain coordinates, severity, and description to warn fellow city residents. You may submit reports anonymously.
  • Audio Recordings: Voice notes recorded for citizen hazard reports are processed locally and encoded as web audio data. We do not use your voice data for model training or biometric recognition.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver real-time urban telemetry, City Life communicates directly with the following trusted open-source and scientific APIs:

  • Open-Meteo: Non-commercial, privacy-respecting atmospheric telemetry & Air Quality Index (AQI) calculations.
  • OpenStreetMap & Nominatim: Global cartographic mapping tiles and geocoding services.
  • OSRM (Open Source Routing Machine): Pedestrian safe routing calculations.
  • Wikipedia / MediaWiki API: Historical landmark descriptions and cultural heritage summaries.

4. Your Rights Under GDPR & CCPA

Regardless of your geographic location, City Life guarantees you the following rights:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You can export all your saved data, reports, and subscription details in machine-readable JSON format directly from your User Profile screen.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can permanently delete your account, session, and all associated local caches in one click via the User Profile screen.
  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, lease, or monetize your personal data or location coordinates to third-party ad brokers.
