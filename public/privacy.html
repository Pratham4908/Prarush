Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 | Effective for all global users of Prarush

1. Executive Commitment

Prarush is engineered with a local-first, zero-tracking architecture. We believe your productivity workflows, notes, code snippets, project sprints, and uploaded documents belong exclusively to you. We do not sell user data, train proprietary foundation models on your private documents, or employ third-party advertising trackers.

2. Data Collection & Storage Architecture

  • Client-Side Local Storage: By default, all workspaces, Kanban sprint tasks, markdown notes, calendar events, and document metadata reside locally in your browser's persistent storage.
  • Authentication & Passwords: Passwords are never stored in plaintext. Accounts use client-side salted SHA-256 cryptographic hashing (Web Crypto API) and cryptographically random session tokens. Guest sessions require zero personal identifiable information.
  • Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK): When you configure cloud API keys (OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic), these keys are stored exclusively in your local device storage. Inference requests route directly from your client to the respective provider's HTTPS endpoint without passing through intermediate proxy logging.

3. Third-Party AI Providers

When you trigger AI completion features with external provider keys:

  • OpenAI: Subject to OpenAI Privacy Policy. Per enterprise terms, API data is not used for model training.
  • Google DeepMind: Subject to Google API Services User Data Policy.

4. GDPR & CCPA User Rights

In full compliance with European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to Data Portability (Article 20): You can export 100% of your user-owned data at any time in machine-readable JSON format via the Profile drawer (including workspaces, tasks, notes, documents, events, and automations).
  • Right to Erasure (Article 17): 1-click origin-safe data purge allows permanent deletion of all Prarush data records from your device without affecting other site cookies.
  • Right to Rectification: All notes, task records, and user profiles can be directly edited or deleted in real time.

5. Security Standards

We mandate HTTPS transport layer security (TLS 1.3), Content Security Policy (CSP) headers, and strict origin isolation. Service Workers operate strictly under standard W3C offline cache sandboxing.
